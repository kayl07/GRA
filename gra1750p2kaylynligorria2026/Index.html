Best Chocolate Chip Cookies

Welcome to Sunshine Bakery™! These delicious chocolate chip cookies are soft, sweet, and filled with chocolate chips. They are perfect for sharing with family and friends. Enjoy ‘a little taste of happiness’ with every bite!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons hot water
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Cream together the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar.
  3. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then add the vanilla extract.
  4. Dissolve the baking soda in hot water and add it to the mixture along with the salt.
  5. Stir in the flour, chocolate chips, and chopped walnuts.
  6. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto an ungreased cookie sheet.
  7. Bake for approximately 10 minutes, or until the edges are lightly browned.
  8. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.
